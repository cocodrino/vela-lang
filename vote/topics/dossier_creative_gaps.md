For each candidate atom, the committee evaluates against:

2. **Cross-Lingual Frequency:** Semantic concept appears in top 1000 words across 5+ languages
3. **Phonotactic Compactness:** 1-2 syllables, (C)V, no violations
4. **Semantic Uniqueness:** Not a transparent compound of existing roots
