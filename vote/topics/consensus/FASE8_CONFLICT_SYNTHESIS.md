## 🔴 Major Conflicts (6 words)

### 1. grass = glasa / gras / verdi / prata

| Specialist | Verdict | Form | Argument |
|-----------|---------|------|----------|
| Fonólogo | AMENDED | glasa | Add vowel to glas → /gla.sa/ |
| Lexicógrafo | REJECTED | gras | Missing /r/ in original; "glas" = glass in Germanic |
| Morfólogo | AMENDED | gras | False friend with Germanic "glas" = glass |
| Semanticista | APPROVED | glas | Internally monosemous; external false-friend only |
| Esteticista | REJECTED | verdi | "glas lacks sunlit diffusion"; proposes verdi /prata |

**User question:** ¿gras (Germanic, faithful to English), glasa (phonotactic fix), o verdi/prata (Romance alternatives)?

### 2. green = grini / grin / gren / grene

| Specialist | Form | Argument |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Fonólogo | grini | Q3 vowel-final; epenthetic /i/ |
| Morfólogo | grin | English /iː/ = single i (tree→tri, see→si) |
| Lexicógrafo | gren | Single 'e' regularizes; double-ee contradicts VELA pattern |
| Esteticista | grene | /e/ pairs with "morne" as dawn-and-dew diptych; softens /gr/ growl |

**User question:** ¿grin, gren, grini, o grene? Criterio: ¿fonética pura, ortografía simple, o estética poética?
