# Consensus: self-nof — Consciousness

**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Topic:** Should `self-nof` (self + know) mean "consciousness" in VELA?
**Committee:** 5 specialists
**Status:** ⚠️ STRONG CONSENSUS with one substantive dissent

---

## Specialist Votes

| Specialist | Verdict | Confidence | Key Point |
|------------|---------|------------|-----------|
| **Phonologist** | ✅ APPROVE | High | /self.nof/ phonotactically clean; iambic rhythm; /f/ echo aesthetically pleasing |
| **Morphologist** | ✅ APPROVE | High | `self-` is prefix, so `self-nof` correctly hyphenated; `selfnof` would break convention |
| **Lexicographer** | ✅ APPROVE | Med-High | Beats `konshusnes` decisively; pan-recognizable via Germanic + Romance traditions |

**Tally:** 4 APPROVE / 1 MODIFY (not reject)

---

## Unanimous Agreements (all 5)

1. `self-nof` is **phonotactically and morphologically valid** as a compound.
2. `self-nof` must be **hyphenated** (`self-nof`), not fused (`selfnof`), because `self-` is a prefix.
3. An **atomic loan** like `konshusnes` is inferior — violates atom ceiling, requires nativizing /ʃ/, and loses recognizability.
4. `self-nof` sounds **pleasant and orthographically clean**.

---

## The Conflict: Meaning Assignment

**Majority view (4/5):** `self-nof` = "consciousness" is acceptable via metonymy, following German *Selbstbewusstsein* and the philosophical tradition of equating self-knowledge with awareness of being.

**Semanticist dissent:** The metonymic leap violates VELA's transparency principles. `self-nof` literally composes to "self-knowledge" (eventive), not "consciousness" (stative). Assigning it to "consciousness" creates polysemy risk and breaks parallelism with `nof-nes` (knowledge).

**Semanticist alternative:**
- `self-nof` = "self-knowledge" (literal, transparent)
- `mind-wek` = "consciousness" (mind + awake = state of being mentally awake/aware)

Both `mind` and `wek` are **existing roots** in LEXICON_BASE.md:
- `mind` = /maind/ — mind
- `wek` = /wek/ — to wake up

---

## Tie-Breaker Recommendation

**Default to majority (4/5):** Approve `self-nof` = "consciousness" with the following annotations:
- Document that `self-nof` carries a **philosophical register** (awareness of one's own existence)
- Note: clinical/wakefulness "consciousness" may need a separate term if ever required
- The semanticist's `mind-wek` is **archived as an alternative** if polysemy problems emerge in usage

**Rationale for overriding semanticist:** The morphologist, lexicographer, and aestheticist all evaluated the word AS "consciousness" and found it acceptable. The semanticist's concern is valid but theoretical — no actual polysemy conflict exists yet in VELA's corpus. Germanic languages have lived with *Selbstbewusstsein* → "consciousness" for centuries.
