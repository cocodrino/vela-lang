## Conflicts the orchestrator must surface

1. **still — the `yeta` votes rest on a premise two specialists refuted.** S, A and L voted to coin because `stila` = steal. P and M showed `/l/` is a soft coda (`GRAMMAR_COMPLETE.md` §6.1), so `stil` never takes `-a` and never collides. Verified: `stil` is already used with the "still/yet" sense in `LEXICON_EXPANSION.md:552` ("Li es stil no-marri") and `:889`, and as "motionless" in `SHORT_STORY_BIRD.md:25`. The validator flags only the English spelling `still` (double `l`).
2. **solitude — no majority.** `alon` is attested in the corpus (`STORIES_SET_01.md:8`, `SHORT_STORY_BIRD.md:75`) and `-nes` is productive (`rich-nes`, `long-nes`, `happi-nes`). The aestheticist dissents: a poem's key word should be one atom (`solituda`).
3. **lost — the tie hides two different questions.** Paraphrase fixes this one sentence; `losta` gives VELA an adjective for "lost" in general (lost object, lost cause).
