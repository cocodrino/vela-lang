## Notes per word

- **much (§6.3)** — VELA should NOT encode a mass/count split in the quantifier. Countability belongs to the noun; Mandarin 多 does not split it. Consistent with the July reform direction (no passive, unmarked present, invariable adjectives). Risk: mild bleaching of `meni`, the same drift English "much" went through.
- **still (§6.2)** — VELA needs it: aspectual mirror of existing `olredi` (done before now vs ongoing now). `stila` = steal is a real homonymy risk. `Mi yeta wait-a.`
- **about** — keep it to the topic sense ("think/talk about X"); "approximately" must get its own form later.
- **lost (§6.4)** — "I'm lost" is a lexicalized state (like `tireda`, `afreda`, `sika`), not a live passive of `luza`. List it as an independent adjective, never derived from `luza` in the grammar. `Mi es losta.`
- **ahead** — "go straight ahead" = "go straight forward"; monosemy: no near-synonym.
- **solitude** — solitude = state of being alone → `alon` + productive `-nes` (cf. `tru-nes`, `nof-nes`). `Bot in alon-nes mi herta lern-a peis.`
- **stick** — a stick is a shape (thin rod), not the material; `wuda` loses the meaning.
