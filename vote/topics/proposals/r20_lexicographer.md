## Notes

- **A1 — dossier understates free `lait`:** it is also used free in the lexicons' own examples: `LEXICON_EXPANSION.md:813,1147,1152,1553,1583`, `LEXICON_EXTENDED.md:198`. Illumination is the dominant sense and deserves its own clean root.
  - *Orchestrator check:* verified, plus `tu-wei` (`:1220`) and `balba` (`LEXICON_BASE.md:1002`). But `LEXICON_EXPANSION.md:74` `La gas es lait.` uses free `lait` for **weight**.
  - *Orchestrator note:* pairing free `luma` with bound `lait-` breaks R7, where the bound form is the citation minus `-a` (`luma` → `lum-`). It is suppletion, a new exception class.
- **`luma`:** Latin lumen/lux → es/it/pt luz/luce, en illuminate/lumen, loaned in ja/ru. Not in any lexicon (verified).
- **`karda`:** Latin charta → fr carte, es/it carta, en card, de Karte, ru карта. Minimal pair with `karta` (constitution).
- **`net`:** English "net" and Spanish "red" both cover mesh and network — ordinary polysemy.
