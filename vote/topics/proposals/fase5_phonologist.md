# Phonologist Review: Fase 5 Full Lexicon

**Reviewer:** Phonologist (pi-73824)  
**Date:** 2026-06-17  
**Scope:** 232 words across 23 domains  
**Lens:** Strict phonotactics — (C)V syllable structure, word-final codas limited to /n m l r/, no /θ ð ʒ/, penultimate pitch accent.

---

## Executive Summary

- **Tier A (Approve as-is):** ~85 words — clean open syllables or legal single-coda finals (/n m l r/).
- **Tier B (Approve with fix):** ~115 words — end in a single non-tolerated coda (/d g p t k s f v ʃ/). Repair: add final /a/ or /o/ to close the syllable legally.
- **Tier C (Reject / Propose alternative):** ~32 words — contain impossible final clusters (-tm, -mnt, -sm, -shn, -bl, -ks, -nd, -rs, -lt, -st, etc.).

**Systemic finding:** The Phase 5 draft relies heavily on raw loan endings. A simple vowel-harmony rule (add /a/ or /o/ to terminal consonants) would bring ~70% of Tier B words into compliance without semantic loss. The cluster violations in Tier C require structural repair.

## Systemic Phonological Issues

1. **Terminal consonant epidemic:** ~115 words (50% of lexicon) end in a single consonant that is not /n m l r/. This suggests VELA needs a formal "thematic vowel" rule for loan adaptation — recommend default /a/ (e.g., `kod` → `koda`, `bug` → `buga`).

2. **Coda cluster compression:** 32 words compress English final clusters into illegal VELA codas: /tm/ (`algoritm`, `ritm`, `kwantm`, `momentm`), /mnt/ (`elemnt`, `tretmnt`, `parlamnt`), /sm/ (`simbolism`), /ʃn/ (`vershn`, `fikshn`), /bl/ (`varibl`, `tebl`), /ks/ (`indeks`, `paradoks`), /st/ (`past`, `aktivist`), /rd/ (`kord`), /rs/ (`fors`, `univers`), /tr/ (`karektr`, `teattr`), /ns/ (`transparens`), /nt/ (`not` in `konsent` if simplified), etc.

3. **Grandfathered Phase 4 violations:** `fors`, `art`, `self`, `god`, `hous` (if /s/ final) contain codas that are now explicitly illegal under the (C)V + /n m l r/ rule. Recommend evolving these to `forsa`, `arta`, `sela`, `goda`, `housa` in new compounds, with a 2-release deprecation window.

4. **Penultimate accent ambiguity:** Monosyllabic loans (`kod`, `bug`, `loop`, `chip`, `drum`, `klon`, `jen`, `kor`, `luv`, `kea`) have no penult. This is not a violation per se, but it weakens the prosodic system. Recommend avoiding new monosyllabic atoms where possible.

5. **/θ ð ʒ/ inventory:** Clean. No violations found in Phase 5.

---

## Top 5 Recommendations

1. **Adopt default thematic vowel /a/ for loan adaptation.** Every word ending in an illegal single coda gets /a/: `koda`, `buga`, `loopa`, `chipa`, `kompaila` (wait, `kompail` ends in /l/ — already legal). This single rule fixes ~70% of Tier B without semantic loss.

2. **Ban the following final clusters outright:** -tm, -mnt, -sm, -shn, -bl, -ks, -st, -rd, -rs, -tr, -ns, -nt. Any loan containing them must be restructured with a terminal vowel or replaced with a transparent compound.

3. **Formalize the `-nes` → `-nesa` rule.** When `-nes` stands alone or closes a phrase, it becomes `-nesa`. In compounds it may remain `-nes` (`tru-nes` as modifier), but standalone abstract nouns should be `trunesa`, `nofnesa`.

4. **Repair the 5 most compressed forms immediately:** `tretmnt` → `tretmenta` or `kura`; `parlamnt` → `parlamenta`; `govrnr` → `govrna`; `elemnt` → `elemen` or `elementa`; `algoritm` → `algoritmo`. These are the worst offenders and create a poor phonological impression.

5. **Create a Phase 4 errata for grandfathered codas.** Update `fors` → `forsa`, `art` → `arta`, `self` → `sela` in all new compounds. Allow 2-release coexistence period.
