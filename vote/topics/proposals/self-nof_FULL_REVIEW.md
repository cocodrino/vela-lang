## Systemic Issues Identified

### Issue 1: Loan Overload (CRITICAL)
- **Data:** 145 of 232 words (62%) are loans.
- **Most affected domains:** Ethics (100%), Politics (100%), Visual Arts (90%), Literature (85%), Film (88%).
- **Impact:** Violates the compound-first philosophy. Threatens the 200-atom soft ceiling.
- **Recommendation:** Phase 6 should systematically replace unjustified loans with compounds, starting with Ethics, Politics, and Visual Arts.

### Issue 2: 3-Root Compound Limit
- **Data:** 5 compounds use 3 roots: `big-muzik-grup`, `far-si-tool`, `past-luv-pain`, `film-rul-po`, `muzik-mak-po`.
- **Verdict:** All 5 are semantically justified but at the limit. No 4-root compounds exist (good).
- **Recommendation:** Monitor closely. If a concept needs 4 roots, prefer a loan.

### Issue 3: Orthography Inconsistency
- **Data:** §15.1 says root+root = no hyphen. Entire lexicon uses hyphens.
- **Affected:** All compounds.
- **Recommendation:** Schedule a dedicated orthography deliberation. Options: (A) enforce fusion, (B) adopt universal hyphenation as de facto standard.

### Issue 4: Suffix Consistency
- **`-nes` suffix:** Used for `tru-nes` (truth), `nof-nes` (knowledge). Should more abstract nouns use this?
- **`-po` suffix:** Used for `muzik-mak-po`, `film-rul-po`, `vote-po`. `muzik-mak-po` and `film-rul-po` are structurally excessive.
- **Recommendation:** Document `-nes` and `-po` as productive suffixes. Restrict `-po` to 2-root max.

### Issue 5: Undefined / Ambiguous Entries
- **`kea`:** Etymology unclear. Meaning vague. Should be replaced.
- **`luv`:** Appears as both approved atomic and Phase 4 errata. Status must be clarified.
- **`spiritu`:** `spirit` + `-u` suffix, but `spirit` itself is a loan. Morphologically redundant.
