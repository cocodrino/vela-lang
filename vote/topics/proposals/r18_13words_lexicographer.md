## Notes per word

- **much** — no pan-recognizable root splits much/many across 3+ language families; `meni` covers both, `hou` handles degree. Risk: loses the English much/many distinction. `Hou meni mani yu hav?`
- **still** — `jeta` fails the lexicographer's own ≥3-language-group bar (English-only anchor). Fallback: paraphrase. Flagged to aestheticist.
- **lost** — R12 bans the passive, so `luz-ed` as a state is out. Risk: "lost object / lost cause" may need a `luza`-derived form later.
- **behind** — `baki-wei` can also read "backward".
- **out** — prefers `out-` because `outputa` is "newer/more frequent"; explicitly defers the call to the phonologist (diphthong representation).
- **solitude** — `solo` is pan-recognizable (es/it/pt solo, fr seul/solitaire, en solo/sole). `-nes` is productive (`sik-nes`, `fren-nes`). `sola-nes` must stay neutral/peaceful, distinct from negative `lonli-*`. `Bot in sola-nes mi herta lern-a peis.`
- **stick** — risk: conflates "a stick" with "wood" as material.
