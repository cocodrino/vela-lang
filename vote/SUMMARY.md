## What This Means for VELA

VELA now has a principled, committee-approved vocabulary architecture:

- **200 atoms** = the empirically validated core for human communication
- **Quality Gate** = the active filter that determines compound vs. atom status
- **Existing lexicon** = grandfathered (no text breaks)
- **Future growth** = guided by semantic necessity, not arbitrary numbers

This prevents VELA from becoming:
- **Too sparse** (Toki Pona: ~120 words, too limited for daily life)
- **Too bloated** (Esperanto: ~900+ roots, memorization wall)

VELA stays in the **IAL sweet spot**: learnable, expressive, and beautiful.
