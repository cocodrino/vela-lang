The one rule: es = am / is / are

One word for “to be” — and it never changes

In English you juggle am, is, are. In VELA there is just es, the same for everyone. The order is Subject — es — the rest.

VELAEnglish
Mi es hapi.I am happy.
Yu es mi fren.You are my friend.
Li es gud.He / she is good.

To make it negative, put no before it: Mi no es sori. — I am not sorry.

Dialogue — Tu fren

A
Helo! Mi neim es Ana.
Hello! My name is Ana.
B
Helo, Ana! Mi es Ben.
Hello, Ana! I am Ben.
A
Yu es mi fren. Mi es hapi!
You are my friend. I am happy!
B
Mi es hapi tu!
I am happy too!

The one rule: la & un

“the” and “a” — that’s all the articles there are

la means the (a specific one). un means a / an (any one). They never change for gender or number.

VELAEnglish
la moterthe mother
un frena friend
un big familia big family

Adjectives go before the noun and never change: un smol child — a small child. la old man — the old man.

Dialogue — Mi famili

A
Dis es mi famili. Mi hav un moter and un fater.
This is my family. I have a mother and a father.
B
La moter es yong. La fater es old!
The mother is young. The father is old!
A
Ha! Mi hav un smol broter tu.
Ha! I have a small brother too.
B
Un big famili. Nais!
A big family. Nice!
