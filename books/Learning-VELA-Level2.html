The one rule: -an = “in progress”

Add -an to the short root for an action happening right now

In Level 1 the present just meant “I do this”. To stress that it is happening this very moment, add -an to the short root.

Plain presentIn progress (-an)
Mi sing. (I sing)Mi sing-an. (I am singing)
Li werka. (he works)Li werk-an. (he is working)
Wi kuka. (we cook)Wi kuk-an. (we are cooking)

For the past-in-progress, stack it after -ed: Mi werk-ed-an. — I was working.

Dialogue — Wat yu don-an?

A
Helo! Wat yu don-an nau?
Hello! What are you doing now?
B
Mi kuk-an dina. And yu?
I’m cooking dinner. And you?
A
Mi stod-an VELA! La child-en ple-an autsida.
I’m studying VELA! The kids are playing outside.
B
Nais. Wen mi werk-ed-an, mi no si-ed de.
Nice. When I was working, I didn’t see them.

Your turn

Exercise 1 · make it “in progress”

1. Mi rida. (I read) → I am reading

Mi rid-an.

2. Li dansi. (she dances) → she is dancing

Li dans-an.
Exercise 2 · past-in-progress

I was singing.

Mi sing-ed-an.

The one rule: modal takes the tense, the main verb stays short

kan / mas / wan + short root

The little verb (kan, mas, wan) carries the time; the action verb after it stays in its short root form.

VELAEnglish
Mi kan swim.I can swim.
Yu mas werk.You must work.
Li wan kom.She wants to come.
Mi kan-ed si li.I could see her. (past on kan)

To say you cannot, put no before the modal: Mi no kan draiv. — I can’t drive.

Dialogue — Yu kan helpa mi?

A
Mi mas go a la siti, bot mi no kan draiv.
I must go to the city, but I can’t drive.
B
Mi kan draiv! Mi wan helpa yu.
I can drive! I want to help you.
A
Tanki! Yu es un veri gud fren.
Thanks! You are a very good friend.
B
Wi mas go nau. La siti es far.
We must go now. The city is far.

Your turn

Exercise 1 · translate to VELA

1. I can cook.

Mi kan kuk.

2. We must study.

Wi mas stodi. (stodi already ends short)

3. She wants to sing.

Li wan sing.
Exercise 2 · say it about you

Name one thing you can do and one you must do today.

e.g. Mi kan swim. Nau-dei mi mas werk.

The one rule: mor = -er, mos = -est

Put mor or mos before the adjective

No word changes its ending — you just add a little word in front. After es the adjective is in its full form (Level 1, R10): biga, guda.

VELAEnglish
La kar es fasta.The car is fast.
La kar es mor fasta.The car is faster.
Dis kar es mos fasta.This car is the fastest.

Two handy pairs: mor guda = better, mor bada = worse. To say “than”, use from: Mi es mor talla from yu. — I am taller than you.

Dialogue — Hu es mor fasta?

A
Mi-se doga es veri fasta!
My dog is very fast!
B
Ha! Mi-se doga es mor fasta from yu-se.
Ha! My dog is faster than yours.
A
No! Wi mas si. Hu-se doga es mos fasta?
No! We must see. Whose dog is the fastest?
B
Wi ran de nau. Den wi nof!
Let’s race them now. Then we’ll know!

Your turn

Exercise 1 · compare

1. The book is heavier. (heavy = hevi)

La buka es mor hevi.

2. This dog is the strongest.

Dis doga es mos strong.

3. I am taller than you.

Mi es mor talla from yu.

The one rule: two small sentences → one bigger one

Put the joining word between the two ideas

VELAEnglish
Mi werka bikos mi wan moni.I work because I want money.
If yu kom, den wi ple.If you come, then we play.
Mi es tireda, bot mi es hapi.I am tired, but I am happy.
La rein kom, so wi stei hom.The rain comes, so we stay home.

tuo = although: Mi werka tuo mi es tireda. — I work although I’m tired.

Dialogue — Wai yu no kom?

A
Wai yu no kom-ed yestdei?
Why didn’t you come yesterday?
B
Bikos mi es-ed veri tireda. Mi werk-ed ol la dei.
Because I was very tired. I worked all day.
A
Mi nof. Bot if yu kom tomoro, den wi eata.
I understand. But if you come tomorrow, then we’ll eat.
B
Ye! Tomoro mi no werk, so mi kan kom.
Yes! Tomorrow I don’t work, so I can come.

Your turn

Exercise 1 · join the two ideas

“I eat.” + “I am hungry.” (because)

Mi eata bikos mi es hagri.

“You help me.” + “I am happy.” (if… then)

If yu helpa mi, den mi es hapi.
Exercise 2 · your reason

Finish: Mi liki VELA bikos…

e.g. …bikos li es simpli and nais. (…because it is simple and beautiful.)

The one rule: sequence words open the clause

Start with the time word, then a normal sentence

VELAEnglish
Firsta mi eata, den mi werka.First I eat, then I work.
Bifor mi slipa, mi rida un buka.Before I sleep, I read a book.
Aft la werka, wi go hom.After work, we go home.

Mix in your Level-1 tenses: Wen mi es-ed child, mi ple-ed ol dei. — When I was a child, I played all day.

Dialogue — Mi-se dei

A
Wat yu don in moring?
What do you do every morning?
B
Firsta mi drinka kofi, den mi go a la werka.
First I drink coffee, then I go to work.
A
And aft la werka?
And after work?
B
Mi go hom, eata dina, and bifor mi slipa mi rida.
I go home, eat dinner, and before I sleep I read.

Your turn

Exercise 1 · order the day

“First I wake up, then I eat.”

Exercise 2 · your morning, in order

Describe your morning using firsta… den… aft…

e.g. Firsta mi weka, den mi drinka kofi, aft mi go a la werka.

The one rule: a = to, from = from

Movement uses the same little words you know

VELAEnglish
Mi go a la siti.I go to the city.
Li kom from hom.She comes from home.
Turna lefa at la strita.Turn left at the street.
La shop es neer, no far.The shop is near, not far.

Giving an order (a command) is easy — just start with the verb: Go raia! — Go right! · Kom hier! — Come here!

Dialogue — Wer es la lern-hous?

A
Wer es la lern-hous? Li es far?
Where is the school? Is it far?
B
No, li es neer! Go raia, den turna lefa.
No, it’s near! Go right, then turn left.
A
Tanki! La lern-hous es insaida la park?
Thanks! Is the school inside the park?
B
No, li es autsida, neer la river.
No, it’s outside, near the river.

Your turn

Exercise 1 · translate to VELA

1. I come from the city.

Mi kom from la siti.

2. Turn right, then go home.

Turna raia, den go hom.
Exercise 2 · give a direction

Tell a friend how to get from the school to your home.

e.g. Go raia, turna lefa at la big strita, mi-se hous es neer la river.

The big idea: VELA lets you build words like bricks

Three tiny endings unlock hundreds of words

PieceExampleMeaning

-fulkolor-fulcolourful
-fulhop-fulhopeful

You don’t memorise every word — you make it. gud → gud-nes (goodness). strong → strong-nes (strength).

Dialogue — La biju-nes of la siti

A
Dis siti es veri nais!
This city is very beautiful!
B
Ye. Mi luva la biju-nes of la old hous-en.
Yes. I love the beauty of the old houses.
A
La strita-n es kolor-ful and la man-en es hop-ful.
The streets are colourful and the people are hopeful.
B
Tru! Mi no-hapi wen mi mas go.
True! I’m unhappy when I have to leave.

Take an action or thing, add -po (person), and you have the job.

Useful pattern: saying what hurts

“My ___ has pain”

Use hava pain (have pain) with the body part, or just es sika (is sick).

VELAEnglish
Mi-se heada hava pain.My head hurts.
Mi es sika nau-dei.I am sick today.
Yu mas si un dokter.You must see a doctor.

Body parts make plurals normally: tu handa-n (two hands), tu futa-n (two feet).

Dialogue — At la dokter

A
Dokter, mi-se heada hava pain and mi es veri tireda.
Doctor, my head hurts and I’m very tired.
B
Yu drinka som watre? Yu mas slipa mor.
Do you drink some water? You must sleep more.
A
Ye, mi werk-ed ol la wik.
Yes, I worked all week.
B
Den rest nau. Tomoro yu es mor guda.
Then rest now. Tomorrow you’ll be better.

Your turn

The one rule: dat links a thought

Verb of the mind + dat + a whole sentence

VELAEnglish
Mi tinka dat li es guda.I think that it is good.
Mi hopa dat yu kom.I hope that you come.
Mi bilever dat VELA es simpli.I believe that VELA is simple.
Mi agria wit yu.I agree with you.

Politeness: end a request with plise, and thank with tanki. Giva mi la buka, plise. — Give me the book, please.

Dialogue — Mi tinka dat…

A
Mi tinka dat dis buka es mos guda from ol.
I think that this book is the best of all.
B
Mi no agria! Mi bilever dat la firsta buka es mor guda.
I don’t agree! I believe the first book is better.
A
Ha! Mi hopa dat yu rida dis wan alo.
Ha! Well, I hope that you read this one too.
B
Mi wil. Giva mi li, plise. Tanki!
I will. Give it to me, please. Thanks!

Your turn

Exercise 1 · report the thought

1. I think that the dog is happy.

2. I hope that you are well.

Mi hopa dat yu es guda.
Exercise 2 · your opinion

e.g. Mi tinka dat VELA es simpli and nais. Mi hopa dat meni man-en lern li.

12 Leson ten-tu

Un stori — A story & big review

read a whole storyuse every tensetell your own tale

Read the story — La smol birda

La smol birda and la storma

Wan taim, un smol birda liv-ed in un big tre neer la river. In moring li sing-ed, and la son es-ed warma. Bot wan dei un storma kom-ed. La winda es-ed veri strong, and la smol birda es-ed afreda.

Li tinka-ed: “Mi es smol and wika. Wat mi kan don?” Den un old birda kom-ed and tok-ed: “Yu no es wika. If yu wan, yu kan flai ovar la storma.”

So la smol birda open-ed li-se flai-arm-en and flai-ed. Li flai-ed mor hai from la storma, and dar la son es-ed nais. Aft la storma, un big rein-arka kom-ed. La smol birda es-ed veri hapi, bikos nau li nof-ed dat li es strong.

Every tense is here: liv-ed (past), es / kan (present/modal), kan flai (modal + root), sing-ed (past). Read it aloud twice!
