# VELA Conlang Project — Research Index

> Comprehensive research documentation for the VELA constructed language project.
> These documents form the theoretical and typological foundation for VELA's design across phonology, orthography, semantics, morphology, lexicon, and history.

---

## 📚 Research Documents

### 1. [Phonology Deep Dive](./01-phonology.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~17,000 words**

How natural languages design their sound systems — cross-linguistic survey of 20+ languages, natural classes of sounds, phonotactic patterns, co-occurrence constraints, what makes phonology feel organic vs. constructed. Includes VELA-specific recommendations for consonant/vowel inventories, phonotactics, allophonic rules, tone, stress, and phonological "signature" features.

**Key topics:** UPSID/UCLA phonological databases, sonority hierarchy, syllable structure typology, vowel harmony, gemination, natural classes (coronals, obstruents, sonorants), allophonic variation, tonal systems.

---

### 2. [Writing Systems](./02-writing-systems.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~17,000 words**

How to design a naturalistic script from scratch — writing system typology (logographic, syllabic, alphabetic, abjad, abugida, featural), case studies of invented scripts (Cherokee syllabary 1820s, Hangul 1446, Zhuyin 1900s), ancient vs. modern script aesthetics, script evolution and orthographic reform, designing VELA's script with practical recommendations.

**Key topics:** Sequoyah's Cherokee syllabary design principles, King Sejong's featural Hangul system, Zhuyin's phonetic notation, script directionality, inscriptional vs. cursive forms, orthographic reform.

---

### 3. [Semantic Typology](./03-semantic-typology.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~17,800 words**

How different cultures categorize reality differently from English — Berlin & Kay color term hierarchy, spatial metaphors and cardinal vs. relative direction systems, tense-aspect systems, evidentiality (direct/inferential/reported), classifier systems and noun classes, avoiding Anglocentrism in semantic design, VELA-specific non-Anglocentric recommendations for each domain.

**Key topics:** Berlin & Kay universal color hierarchy, WALS evidentiality typology, classifier languages (Chinese, Japanese, Mayan), relative vs. cardinal spatial reference frames, perfective/imperfective aspect, motion framing (satellite vs. verb-framed).

---

### 4. [Conlang History & Case Studies](./04-conlang-history.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~25,600 words**

Deep dive into 7 influential constructed languages: Esperanto (L.L. Zamenhof, 1887), Klingon (Marc Okrand, 1984), Dothraki (David J. Peterson, 2009), High Valyrian (Peterson, 2011), Toki Pona (Sonja Lang, 2001), Ithkuil (John Quijada, 1978-2004), Láadan (Suzette Elgin, 1982). Design philosophy, what works, what doesn't, and specific lessons for VELA from each.

**Key topics:** Agglutinative vs. polysynthetic morphology, naturalistic vs. a priori design, Sapir-Whorf testing, Klingon ejective phonology, Dothraki Turkic vowel harmony inspiration, High Valyrian Latin/Greek aesthetic, Toki Pona radical minimalism, Ithkuil extreme complexity, Láadan feminist language.

---

### 5. [Sound Symbolism](./05-sound-symbolism.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~18,400 words**

The research on phonosemantics and non-arbitrary sound-meaning mapping — the Bouba/Kiki effect (Takete/Maluma), cross-linguistic sound symbolism studies, vowel symbolism (high vowels = small, front = feminine, back = masculine), consonant symbolism (fricatives = sharp/cutting, nasals = continuous), English phonaesthemes (gl-, sn-, sl-, fl-), crossmodal correspondences, ideophones, systematic application in VELA's lexicon.

**Key topics:** Ramachandran & Hubbard (2001) Bouba/Kiki replication, Ohala's frequency code hypothesis, phonaestheme typology, articulatory mapping hypothesis, cross-cultural consistency of sound symbolism, phonosemantic map for VELA.

---

### 6. [Language Evolution](./06-language-evolution.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~17,400 words**

How languages change over time — Grimm's law (First Germanic Sound Shift), types of sound change (lenition, fortition, assimilation, dissimilation, deletion, epenthesis, metathesis), the Great Vowel Shift, grammaticalization paths (content word → clitic → affix), Proto-Indo-European reconstruction methodology, creating a genealogical branch of VELA daughter languages, semantic change typology.

**Key topics:** Grimm's Law and Verner's Law, Great Vowel Shift, Hopper & Traugott grammaticalization stages, causative > auxiliary > modal paths, case erosion and renewal cycles, word order change (SOV > SVO), semantic narrowing and broadening, archaisms in formal registers.

---

### 7. [Advanced Morphology](./07-advanced-morphology.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~19,000 words**

Advanced morphological systems — polysynthesis and noun incorporation (Inuktitut, Mohawk, Navajo), verb serialization (Mandarin, Ewe, West African languages), switch-reference systems (Amurdag, Mandane), alignment types (nominative-accusative, ergative-absolutive, tripartite, active-stative split-S), inverse systems (Algonquian), case system typology, how languages encode empathy and perspective, practical VELA morphological design.

**Key topics:** Active-stative split-S alignment, Algonquian inverse system, Míanách Amhráinn (Irish) switch-reference, Basque ergativity, Finnish 15-case system, nominal case derivation from postpositions, polypersonal verb agreement, causative-applicative symmetry.

---

### 8. [Lexicography Methods](./08-lexicography.md)
**Author:** Deep Research Subagent | **~22,200 words**

How to build 1,000+ words that don't feel like English translations — root creation algorithms, compounding strategies, semantic field organization, affixation patterns, avoiding Anglocentrism, creating untranslatable words, number systems typology (quinary, decimal, vigesimal), pronoun systems (inclusive/exclusive we, T-V politeness distinction), demonstratives, Zipf's law and word frequency, quality control testing, sample VELA root lexicon (100 core words).

**Key topics:** Semantic field tiering (Tier 1-3 vocabulary), compounding types (endocentric, exocentric, appositional, coordinate), Swadesh list methodology, Vulgar/Lexifer/Awkwords conlang tools, VELA T-V politeness distinction, cultural vocabulary design, false friend avoidance.
