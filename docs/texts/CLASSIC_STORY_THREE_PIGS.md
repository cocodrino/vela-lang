# The Three Little Pigs — VELA Version

**Phase:** 8 (Texts and Samples)
**Date:** 2026-05-15

## English (short canonical version)
Three little pigs left home to build their own houses.
The first pig built a house of straw.
The second pig built a house of sticks.
The third pig built a house of bricks.
A wolf came and said, “Little pig, little pig, let me come in.”
The pigs said, “No, no, no.”
The wolf said, “Then I will blow your house down.”
He blew down the straw house and the stick house.
But he could not blow down the brick house.
The pigs stayed safe, and the wolf ran away.

## VELA (working translation)
Tri smol pig-en go-ed from hom tu maki de-se own hous-en.
La first pig maki-ed un hous of stra.
La second pig maki-ed un hous of stik-en.
La third pig maki-ed un hous of brik-en.
Un wulf kom-ed and tok-ed: “Smol pig, smol pig, let mi kom in.”
La pig-en tok-ed: “No, no, no.”
La wulf tok-ed: “Den mi wil blow daun yu-se hous.”
Li blow-ed daun la stra hous and la stik hous.
Bat li no kin blow daun la brik hous.
La pig-en stay-ed sef, and la wulf ran away.
