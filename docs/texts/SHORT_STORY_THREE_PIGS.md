# Tri Smol Pigi

**VELA:**
Wan taim, tri smol pig-en liv-ed wit de-se mat in big fores. Wan dei mat tok-ed, "Yu ar old enuf. Go maki yu on haus."

Pig 1 tok-ed,"Mi wil maki wan kwik haus." Li maki-ed gras-haus. "Dis kwik and esy," li tok-ed.

Pig 2 tok-ed,"Mi haus wil be strong." Li maki-ed wud-haus. "Dis strong," li tok-ed.

Pig 3 tok-ed,"Mi wil maki la strong-moa haus." Li maki-ed ston-haus. It tuk long taim, bot li smile-ed. "Now wulfi no kan kom insaid," li tok-ed.

Big wulfi kom-ed on wan dei. Li si-ed gras-haus and tok-ed laud,"Smol pig, smol pig, let mi in!"

"No, no, not bai la hair on mi hed," Pig 1 tok-ed.

"Den mi wil blo and mi wil blo, and yu haus wil break!" wulfi tok-ed. And li blo-ed, and li blo-ed, and gras-haus flai-ed awe. Pig 1 ran to wud-haus.

Wulfi kom-ed to wud-haus."Smol pig, smol pig, let mi in!"

"No, no, not bai la hair on mi hed," Pig 2 tok-ed.

"Den mi wil blo and mi wil blo, and yu haus wil break!" wulfi tok-ed. And li blo-ed, and wud-haus break-ed. Pig 1 and Pig 2 ran to ston-haus.

"Smol pig, smol pig, let mi in!" wulfi sey-ed.

"No, no, not bai la hair on mi hed," Pig 3 tok-ed.

"Den mi wil blo and mi wil blo, and yu haus wil break!" wulfi tok-ed. Li blo-ed and blo-ed, bot ston-haus no break-ed. Wulfi taird-ed and sit-ed.

Den li haf wan ide. Li klaim-ed on la ston-haus roof and go-ed down la chimni. Bot Pig 3 put-ed hot watre under la chimni. Wulfi faul-ed down in la hot watre.

"Owowow!" Wulfi jump-ed aut and ran awe in tu la fores. Tri smol pig-en dans-ed in la rum and luk-ed la sun. "Wi ar seif," de tok-ed. And de liv-ed hapi evri dei.

---

**English:**
Once upon a time, three little pigs lived with their mother in a big forest. One day their mother said, "You are old enough. Go make your own house."

Pig 1 said, "I will make a quick house." He made a grass house. "This is quick and easy," he said.

Pig 2 said, "My house will be strong." He made a wood house. "This is strong," he said.

Pig 3 said, "I will make the strongest house." He made a stone house. It took a long time, but he smiled. "Now the wolf cannot come inside," he said.

The big wolf came one day. He saw the grass house and said loudly, "Little pig, little pig, let me in!"

"No, no, not by the hair on my head," said Pig 1.

"Then I will blow and I will blow, and your house will break!" said the wolf. And he blew, and he blew, and the grass house flew away. Pig 1 ran to the wood house.

The wolf came to the wood house. "Little pig, little pig, let me in!"

"No, no, not by the hair on my head," said Pig 2.

"Then I will blow and I will blow, and your house will break!" said the wolf. And he blew, and the wood house broke. Pig 1 and Pig 2 ran to the stone house.

"Little pig, little pig, let me in!" said the wolf.

"No, no, not by the hair on my head," said Pig 3.

"Then I will blow and I will blow, and your house will break!" said the wolf. He blew and blew, but the stone house didn't break. The wolf got tired and sat down.

Then he had an idea. He climbed on the stone house roof and went down the chimney. But Pig 3 put hot water under the chimney. The wolf fell down in the hot water.

"Owowow!" The wolf jumped out and ran away into the forest. The three little pigs danced in the room and looked at the sun. "We are safe," they said. And they lived happily ever after.

## Grammatical Notes

| Feature | Example | Decision |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Compound materials** | `gras-haus`, `wud-haus`, `ston-haus` | Materials as transparent compounds — no new atoms needed. |
| **Comparative / superlative** | `strong-moa` (strong-more) | VELA uses analytic comparison via `moa` — `strong-moa` = strongest. |
| **Imperative / refusal** | `"No, no..." \| "let mi in!"` | Negation = `no` + statement. Imperative = bare verb root. |
| **Future threat** | `"mi wil blo and mi wil blo"` | Future tense `-wil` used in spoken threat, matching natural register. |
| **Result construction** | `"yu haus wil break"` | Causative result = intransitive verb (`break`) with context implying agency. |
| **Emphatic repetition** | `"blo and blo"` | Repetition of bare verb root for emphasis — rhythmic license validated. |
| **Reflexive / possessive** | `"de-se mat"` (their mother), `"mi hed"` (my head) | Genitive `-se` for possession tested in narrative context. |
| **Conative / attempt** | `"Wulfi blo-ed and blo-ed, bot..."` | Repeated past action expressing prolonged effort. |
| **Sound / interjection** | `"Owowow!"` | Interjections pass through as-is when iconic/non-linguistic. |

## Gap Words Identified

| # | Provisional VELA | English | Reason | Proposed Resolution |
|---!--|-------|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | `blo` | to blow (air, wind) | No VELA word for "to blow air with mouth" exists. | **New atom** — core verb for fairy tales, weather, and instruments. Short (1 syllable), phonotactically clean (C)V. |
| 2 | `chimni` | chimney | No VELA word for "chimney / smoke passage". | **New atom** or compound `smok-tub` / `fai-tub`. Story needs a functional word; `chimni` is 2 syllables, vowel-final. |
| 3 | `faul` | to fall (downward) | Previous texts avoided this by using `go daun`. Story explicitly needs "fall into water" (involuntary). | **Consideration:** Is `faul` distinct from `go daun`? Semanticist may rule that `go daun` covers all downward motion. If distinct: new atom. If covered: replace with `go daun`. |

**Materials handled as compounds (no new atoms):**
- `gras-haus` = straw/grass house (grass is close semantic substitute for straw)
- `wud-haus` = wood house (sticks are wood)
- `ston-haus` = stone house (bricks are baked stone)

**All other words** in this text were verified as existing in `LEXICON_BASE.md`.
