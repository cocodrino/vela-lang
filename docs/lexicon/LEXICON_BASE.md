## Section 1: Core — Pronouns, Articles, Small Words

*These are the most frequent words in any language. Short, transparent, memorized naturally.*

### Pronouns

| Word | AFI | Type | English | VELA Example | English |
|------|-----|------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **mi** | /mi/ | pronoun | I | Mi go a la siti. | I go to the city. |
| **yu** | /ju/ | pronoun | you (singular) | Yu si la man. | You see the man. |
| **li** | /li/ | pronoun | he/she/it | Li es hapi. | She is happy. |
| **wi** | /wi/ | pronoun | we | Wi liv la siti-te. | We live in the city. |

### Verbs — Extended Set (with example sentences)

This section shows complete example sentences demonstrating all grammar structures.

**Simple present (SVO):**
```
Mi si la film.          → I see the film.
Yu lik la sonj.          → You like the song.
Li wok in la ofis.       → She works in the office.
Wi liv la siti-te.       → We live in the city.
De kom from la naishon.  → They come from the nation.
```

**Present + object:**
```
Mi si yu.               → I see you.
Yu her la sonj.         → You hear the song.
Li giv mi la buk.       → She gives me the book.
Wi teik la child a skol. → We take the child to school.
```

**Past tense (-ed):**
```
Mi wok-ed la wok.       → I worked.
Yu kom-ed hier.          → You came here.
Li si-ed la film.        → She saw the film.
Wi liv-ed in la siti.    → We lived in the city.
De go-ed a la park.      → They went to the park.
```

**Future tense (-wil):**
```
Mi kom-wil tomoro.       → I will come tomorrow.
Yu si-wil la film.       → You will see the film.
Li wok-wil in la ofis.  → She will work in the office.
Wi liv-wil la siti-te.  → We will live in the city.
```

**Negation (no + verb):**
```
Mi no wok.               → I don't work.
Yu no kom.               → You don't come.
Li no si la man.         → She doesn't see the man.
Wi no hav moni.          → We don't have money.
De no undrstand.          → They don't understand.
```

**Modal verbs (kan/mas/wan + bare root):**
```
Mi kan si.               → I can see.
Yu mas wok.               → You must work.
Li wan kom.               → She wants to come.
Wi no kan go.             → We cannot go.
De mas no don dat.        → They must not do that.
```

**Progressive (-an):**
```
Mi wok-an.               → I am working.
Li kom-an.               → She is coming.
Wi liv-an in la siti.    → We are living in the city.
```

**Questions:**
```
Yu si la film q?         → Do you see the film?
Hu yu si?                → Who do you see?
Wat yu lik?               → What do you like?
Wen de kom?              → When do they come?
Wer wi wok?              → Where do we work?
Hai yu fel?              → How do you feel?
Wai yu wok?              → Why do you work?
Yu wan kom or no?         → Do you want to come or not?
```

**With adjectives:**
```
La big man si la smol child.  → The big man sees the small child.
Un nais-im famili liv hier. → A nice family lives here.
La hapi child sing un sonj. → The happy child sings a song.
La fast kar pas la slow man. → The fast car passes the slow man.
```

**Genitive (-se):**
```
La man-se buk es on la tebul. → The man's book is on the table.
La famili-se hous es big.    → The family's house is big.
Wi liv la siti-se sentr-te.  → We live in the city's center.
Mi si yu-se fren.             → I see your friend.
```

**Locative (-te):**
```
Mi liv la siti-te.            → I live in the city.
Wi wok la ofis-te.            → We work at the office.
La bird fly la skai-te.       → The bird flies in the sky.
```

**Conditional (if... den...):**
```
If yu kom, den mi es hapi.    → If you come, I am happy.
If yu no kom, den mi go.      → If you don't come, then I go.
If li wok-ed, den li hav-ed moni. → If she had worked, then she would have had money.
```
