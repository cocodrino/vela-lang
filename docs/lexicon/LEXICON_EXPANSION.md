## Nature & Environment

> Batch 1. Committee review: semanticist (praised the `-lond` series as gold standard), phonologist/aestheticist ("feels like VELA, not English in disguise"), morphologist (flagged real gaps). Applied consensus: `watre-lond → mar-lond` (island, not "wetland"), `morn → moring` (existing root), `jema → biju-ston` (transparent), dropped season compounds (samr/winter/spring/fol already exist) and `sanda`/`beri` (duplicates). The `-lond`/`-wind` compounds keep the grandfathered native root (Option A).
