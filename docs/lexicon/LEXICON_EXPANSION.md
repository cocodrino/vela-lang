# VELA Lexicon — Expansion (toward 3000)

> **Status:** IN PROGRESS — authored by Claude, committee-reviewed, validated by `scripts/validate_phonology.py`.
> **Base:** 1345 unique entries (LEXICON_BASE + LEXICON_EXTENDED) before expansion.
> **Rules:** every word ends in a vowel or /n m l r s/; no `th`/`zh`; hyphen between morphemes; max 3 roots; prefer transparent compounds over loans.
> **Format:** Word → AFI → Type → English → Formation → Example.

Integrated batches appear below, grouped by domain. Only words that pass the phonotactic validator and are not duplicates of existing entries are kept here.
